6) How Your Proposed Minimum Viable Business Product (MVBP) Meets the Three Objectives of an MVBP (Beachhead)

ObjectivesHow, specifically, does your MVBP meet this objective?
ValueEnd users (clinic patients + irritation-prone segment) get a complete, sensitive-skin cream with stacked tolerability support and hard tolerability evidence: HRIPT ≥98% non-irritation on the finished formula, with pH 5.5 base, plus calmers (panthenol, bisabolol, centella). Value is further validated by the expected 3–4 week improvement window and tracked via NPS day 30/60 and reorder intent.
PayThe economic buyer is willing to pay because the MVBP is sellable and priced realistically for Mexico urban clinics and DTC: retail $280–$350 MXN (anchor $300 MXN) for 50g, and clinic ex-factory $130–$160 MXN so clinics can carry inventory and recommend confidently. Success condition is 50 paying DTC customers and clinic pilot restocks, proving willingness to pay and repeat purchase.
FeedbackCreates a real loop across all roles: End user → NPS day 30 & 60 + photo/redness tracking + adverse reaction monitoring, Champion (dermatologist/clinic) → structured trial feedback + restock decision, and Economic buyer → observes patient tolerability and reorder behavior (day 60–90). This converts evidence + usage into iteration on formulation/positioning and accelerates the next maquila run decision.
