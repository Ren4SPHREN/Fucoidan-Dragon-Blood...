MARKET SEGMENTATION

Original Market Segmentation

Five market segments are analyzed: Cosmetic Manufacturers (natural ingredients), Eco-Conscious Consumers, Dermatologists & Skincare Professionals, Wellness & Beauty Influencers, and Research Institutions.

  • 3. Benefit

  • 4. Urgency of Need

Section 2 — Disciplined Entrepreneurship: 24 Steps

  • 1. Market Segmentation

    Three primary segments: sensitive/reactive skin consumers in premium skincare, clean beauty formulators seeking marine bioactives, and dermatologist-recommended cosmetic brands. Geographic focus on Mexico, USA, and Canada. Segmented by skin condition severity, willingness to pay for hypoallergenic claims, and ingredient transparency demand.

  • 2. Beachhead Market

    Mexican premium skincare consumers aged 25–45 with sensitive or atopic skin, purchasing through specialty retail and direct-to-consumer channels. High unmet need for effective hypoallergenic actives with a local sustainable sourcing story.

  • 3. End User Profile

    Woman, 28–42, urban Mexico (CDMX, MTY, GDL, SLP). Middle-to-upper income. Experienced with skincare routines and frustrated by irritation from synthetic actives. Reads ingredient lists carefully. Willing to pay a premium for science-backed, gentle formulations.

  • 4. Beachhead TAM
  • 5. Persona

    "Valentina" — 34, marketing manager in Monterrey. Rosacea-prone skin. Has cycled through multiple serums that caused flare-ups and now buys exclusively hypoallergenic or dermatologist-tested products. Follows clean beauty content creators and responds to scientific transparency and visible sourcing stories.

  • 6. Life Cycle Use Case

    Valentina sees a targeted Instagram ad featuring sargassum's environmental problem reframed as a skincare solution. She visits the DTC site, reads the fucoidan science brief, and purchases a serum. She notices reduced redness within 3 weeks, becomes a repeat buyer, and shares the discovery with their network. Reorder cycle: 60–90 days.

  • 7. High-Level Specs

    Fucoidan concentration ≥1% w/w in final formula. Purity ≥85% fucoidan content post-purification. O/W emulsion format (serum or cream). COFEPRIS-compliant hypoallergenic certification, dermatologically tested. 24-month shelf life. Airless pump in recyclable packaging. Free of parabens, sulfates, and synthetic fragrance.

  • 8. Quantify Value Proposition

    Comparable imported marine-active serums (La Mer, Murad) are priced at $800–$2,500 MXN. A locally produced fucoidan serum can be positioned at $650–$1,200 MXN with equivalent or superior efficacy claims, sustainable origin, and hypoallergenic certification — delivering a ~30–40% cost advantage over imports while commanding a 3–5× markup over production cost.

  • 9. Next 10 Customers

    1–3: Boutique dermocosmetic clinics in SLP and MTY willing to carry private-label hypoallergenic lines. 4–6: Clean beauty concept stores (Cosm, Tono). 7–8: Wellness spas with branded retail. 9–10: Early DTC customers from the founder's personal network and LinkedIn outreach. All reachable within 90 days of MVBP readiness.

  • 10. Define Core

    Core competency is the extraction and purification of high-purity fucoidan from sargassum at competitive cost, combined with cosmetic formulation know-how for sensitive skin. Defensible through process IP, local raw material access, and regulatory compliance expertise (COFEPRIS).

  • 11. Competitive Position

    Plotted on two axes — ingredient sustainability/traceability (X) and hypoallergenic efficacy (Y). Marinova sits high on efficacy but carries no meaningful sustainability narrative for the MX market. Hi-Q Marine offers high purity but has no local presence. Origin by Ocean leads on sustainability but is not cosmetic-focused. SPHREN occupies the high position on both axes simultaneously.

  • 12. DMU (Decision-Making Unit)

    Initiator and end user: Valentina. Economic buyer: themself (DTC) or clinic procurement manager (B2B). Key influencers: dermatologists and clean beauty content creators. Decision maker: the end user in DTC, or the clinic buyer in B2B. Gatekeepers: COFEPRIS certification and retail buyer approval.

  • 13. Customer Acquisition Process

    Awareness built through Instagram and TikTok science content and dermatologist endorsement. Consideration through DTC website, ingredient education, and reviews. Trial via introductory kit or clinic recommendation. Purchase through DTC or clinic POS. Retention through subscription model and loyalty content. Advocacy through UGC and referral program.

  • 14. Follow-on TAM
  • 15. Business Model

    Hybrid model combining a DTC brand (B2C) with ingredient supply (B2B). Revenue streams include product sales, subscription replenishment, and white-label supply contracts. B2C drives margin; B2B drives volume and validates ingredient credibility with the industry.

  • 16. Pricing Framework

    Fucoidan extract for B2B: $800–$1,500 USD/kg depending on purity and volume. Finished serum for B2C: $650–$1,200 MXN per 30ml unit. Finished cream for B2C: $550–$900 MXN per 50ml unit. Pricing anchored to imported marine-active equivalents and justified by the local sustainability story and hypoallergenic certification premium.

  • 17. LTV (Life-Time Value)
  • 18. Sales Process

    B2C: Content → DTC site visit → product page → checkout → post-purchase sequence → reorder trigger at day 60. B2B: Outbound outreach → sample kit sent → formulation trial period (30–60 days) → technical dossier and pricing proposal → purchase order → onboarding → recurring supply agreement. B2B sales cycle: 60–120 days to close.

  • 19. COCA (Cost of Customer Acquisition)

    B2C estimated COCA: $350–$600 MXN (~$18–$30 USD) via paid social and content. Breakeven at approximately 1.5 purchases. B2B estimated COCA: $4,000–$8,000 MXN (~$200–$400 USD) via direct outreach, samples, and trade events, justified by high LTV. Target blended COCA-to-LTV ratio: less than 1:10.

  • 20. Key Assumptions

    Fucoidan at ≥1% concentration produces measurable, perceivable skin benefits in hypoallergenic formulas. Mexican consumers will pay a premium for locally sourced marine bioactives. Sargassum supply from Mexican coasts is consistent and scalable. COFEPRIS certification is achievable within 12–18 months. B2B cosmetic formulators will accept fucoidan as a substitute for synthetic actives.

  • 21. Test Key Assumptions

    Efficacy: 30-day consumer panel measuring TEWL and redness scores. WTP: conjoint analysis survey with 100+ target users in MX urban centers. Supply: 3-month sargassum collection pilot with a coastal community partner. Regulatory: pre-submission meeting with COFEPRIS consultant to map documentation requirements. B2B acceptance: send samples and technical dossier to 5 Mexican cosmetic formulators and track conversion.

  • 22. MVBP (Minimum Viable Business Product)

    A single fucoidan-based hypoallergenic serum (30ml, ≥1% fucoidan, O/W base, dermatologically tested) sold DTC to 10 paying customers in the beachhead segment, with documented repurchase intent. Must include COFEPRIS-compliant labeling, a functional e-commerce checkout, and a post-purchase feedback loop to validate efficacy perception.

  • 23. Dogs Will Eat Dog Food

    3 dermocosmetic clinics in SLP/MTY agree to trial the product with sensitive-skin patients. First 10 DTC customers reorder within 90 days. At least 2 customers report measurable skin improvement and share unprompted. 1 B2B formulator requests a second sample batch after initial evaluation.

  • 24. Product Plan

    Phase 1 (0–12 mo): MVBP serum launched, COFEPRIS process initiated, 10 pilot customers acquired, sargassum supply chain formalized. Phase 2 (12–24 mo): Full COFEPRIS approval, cream SKU launched, 2 B2B clients onboarded, DTC channel optimized, seed round closed. Phase 3 (24–48 mo): Enter US Hispanic market, expand B2B ingredient supply, file process patent (IMPI), launch subscription model, explore EU clean beauty certification (EWG/Cosmos).
