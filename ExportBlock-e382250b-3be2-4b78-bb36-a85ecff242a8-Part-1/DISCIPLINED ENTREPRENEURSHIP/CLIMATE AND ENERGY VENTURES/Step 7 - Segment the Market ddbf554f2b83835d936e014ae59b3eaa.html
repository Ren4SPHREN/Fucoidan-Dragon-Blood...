11Other Segment ConsiderationsInclude things like regulation, certification needs, channel dependencies, etc.

14Competing SolutionsWhat alternatives exist from the user’s perspective (including “do nothing”)?
15Full-Solution NeedsWhat additional components or partners would be needed to offer a full solution?
